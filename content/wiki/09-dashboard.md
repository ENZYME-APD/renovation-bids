# Dashboard and action items
> The figures at the top, the charts, and what each action item means and opens.

## Figures

| Tile | What it counts |
|---|---|
| Bids in progress | Projects from *lead* to *offer sent*, with the total of their budgets |
| Won | Won projects and their contract value (or budget if no contract value yet) |
| Win rate | Won ÷ (won + lost) |
| Average €/m² | Budget ÷ area, averaged over projects that have both |
| Unpaid invoices | Supplier invoices still outstanding |
| Bid hours | Unpaid pre-contract hours recorded on projects |

## Charts

- **Projects by status**: where the pipeline is.
- **New bids per month**: last 12 months.
- **Budget per m²**: by project. Your benchmark for qualifying leads: a client budget well under your usual €/m² is a warning sign.
- **Won projects: budget, committed and paid**: whether purchasing is tracking the budget.

## Action items

Generated automatically from your data. Click one to go where the work is.

| Item | Appears when | Opens |
|---|---|---|
| Lines without a price | A bid still has unpriced lines | The budget, filtered to **Incomplete only** |
| Chase supplier | A request for quote is due a reminder | The project |
| Review quote | A quote has arrived and nobody has reviewed it | The project |
| Offer expiring | A sent offer expires within 14 days | The project |
| Client request | A client request is still open | The project |
| Invoice due | A supplier invoice with money outstanding is due within 7 days, or overdue | Purchases |
| Late delivery | A sent or confirmed purchase order is past its expected date | Purchases |

Items disappear on their own once the data is fixed: price the lines and *"6 lines without a price"* goes away.

**Recent activity** at the bottom shows the latest changes. All of them are in *Settings → Activity*.
